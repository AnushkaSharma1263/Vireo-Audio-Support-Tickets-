**To:** Neha Kulkarni  **Cc:** Priya Raman, Arjun Mehta  **Re:** First-response SLA breaches

**Short answer.** Breaches are not mainly about people or about the morning team. Since the Indore night shift was
dissolved on 30 June 2025, nobody works overnight chat, email or social. Those tickets wait until about 06:00 IST and
breach almost automatically. The morning team inherits them and carries the blame.

**What the data shows** (18 months, 11,200 tickets after removing 616 duplicates)
- Breach rate was 9% before the reshuffle and 25% since. Outside the hours with no cover it is still 9%, so the day-to-day desk is not the issue.
- 1,581 of the 2,233 breaches since the reshuffle (71%) were tickets created between 22:00 and 06:00 IST. 99.9% of overnight chats breach.
- Morning-shift agents look like 37% breachers. Remove the overnight tickets they inherit and they are at 12%, close to Day shift (9%).
  The eight highest-breach agents are all morning-shift, and each drops to about 10% or lower once the overnight tickets are removed.
- Each breach costs Rs 350. Overnight breaches cost about Rs 5.5 lakh a year (about Rs 1.4 lakh a quarter).
- Monthly credits ran about Rs 12k before July 2025 and about Rs 66k since. Volume also grew (new launches), but the breach rate rose too,
  so credits are not flat. This is probably the credit-line jump Arjun asked about. The reshuffle was cost-neutral on payroll, not once the credits are counted.

**Goal.** Cut the overall breach rate from 25% to about 13% within a quarter, worth about Rs 3.8 lakh a year (about Rs 0.95 lakh a quarter),
by covering overnight chat and social. Stretch: about 10% and Rs 4.8 lakh a year if overnight email is covered too.

**Recommendation (no hiring).** Move one existing Day-shift chat agent to a night slot for a four-week trial, and let them also watch overnight email.
Overnight volume is small, about 5 tickets a night, so one person is enough. A new night hire would cost about Rs 4.8 lakh a year and would not pay for itself, which is why this is a move and not a hire.
Day shift loses one of six chat agents. Check Day breaches during the trial; they should stay near 9%.

**Weekly report.** It is attached by agent and by shift. Each breach is marked "staffed hours" or "no cover". Please hold agent conversations on the staffed-hours column only.
That keeps the morning team out of a number they cannot influence, which was Priya's worry.

**What I am not sure of**
- The export has the resolving agent, not who sent the first reply. Agent numbers are therefore a close proxy, not exact.
- Timestamps were read as UTC. Evidence is strong (breach rate jumps at exactly 22:00 IST) but Sameer should confirm.
- Savings assume overnight tickets would breach at the daytime rate (9-13%) once someone is on shift.
- Not covered: CSAT, refunds, replacement cost.

**Checked.** The tool's breach and shift flags match an independent SQL recomputation on all 11,200 tickets (0 differences).
